Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Zero-Log Privacy Standard

Core Privacy Principle: Larpwallet operates on a strict zero-tracking, client-side execution architecture. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any user information or simulator telemetry to remote databases.

This Privacy Policy outlines how data is managed when interacting with the Larpwallet website, standalone Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), and offline simulator tools.

2. Local Device Storage

All customized simulator configurations (including store titles, custom revenue figures, notification counts, crypto asset balances, and keypad values) are saved strictly inside your local browser memory (localStorage).

  • Local Persistence: Settings remain only on your local machine to keep your customized views active between visits.
  • Custom Uploads: Any custom store icons or avatars are converted to local data strings and never uploaded to remote storage.
  • No Server Analytics: No tracking cookies, session recorders, or third-party telemetry tools monitor your usage.

3. Offline Service Worker Caching

The application uses standard browser Service Worker technology to cache static interface assets (fonts, icons, and scripts). This allows all simulators to launch instantly in independent windows and operate 100% offline without communicating over the network.

4. Simulated Forms & Checkout

All login screens, password inputs, and checkout forms throughout the platform are visual simulations. Any sample emails, passwords, or test card numbers entered are processed entirely in local JavaScript and are never transmitted to any payment processor or financial gateway.

5. User Data Erasure

You maintain full ownership and control over your simulator environment. To instantly remove all saved metrics, custom numbers, and cached assets, you can click "Reset" inside any simulator settings panel or clear your browser's local cache and site data.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly to this page. If you have questions regarding our privacy architecture, please contact us via our Discord community.
